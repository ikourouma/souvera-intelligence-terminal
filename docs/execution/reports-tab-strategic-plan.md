## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Reports Tab is **Souvera's strategic differentiator** - where AI-powered intelligence synthesis transforms raw data into actionable investment documents. This plan outlines a 3-phase approach to building a Bloomberg-beating reporting platform while maintaining competitive pricing and superior user experience.

**Key Competitive Advantages**:
1. ✅ **AI-Powered Custom Reports** (GPT-4 synthesis) - Bloomberg has none

## 📧 NEWSLETTER/CURATION SYSTEM (Admin-Managed)

### **Newsletter Architecture**

**Two Newsletter Types**:

1. **Weekly Briefing** (Every Monday, 8 AM local time)
   - **Format**: Email (HTML + plain text)
   - **Length**: 500-800 words
   - **Sections**:
     - 📰 **Key Developments** (3-5 bullet points): Policy changes, economic data, trade updates
     - 🏭 **Sector Alerts** (2-3 bullets): Technology deals, agriculture trends, infrastructure projects
     - ⚠️ **Risk Signals** (1-2 bullets): Currency movements, political events, operational disruptions
     - 🚢 **AGOA Updates** (if applicable): U.S. trade policy changes, product eligibility shifts
   - **CTA**: "Read Full Analysis →" (links to relevant tab in platform)

2. **Monthly Deep-Dive** (First Monday of month, 8 AM local time)
   - **Format**: Email (HTML) + PDF attachment
   - **Length**: 1,500-2,000 words
   - **Sections**:
     - 🔍 **Sector Focus** (rotating): Deep-dive on Technology, Agriculture, Energy, Manufacturing, or Mining
     - 💡 **Investment Themes**: 2-3 emerging opportunities with entry strategies
     - 📜 **Policy Analysis**: Regulatory changes and business implications
     - 📊 **Market Data**: Trade flows, FDI trends, macro indicators
   - **PDF Attachment**: Printable report (4-6 pages)
   - **CTA**: "Download Full Report →" or "Generate Custom Report →"
